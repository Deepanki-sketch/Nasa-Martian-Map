    Welcome to Mars Safe Walk

    This app combines orbital maps with a 3D Martian Street View so an astronaut can plan a walk up Mount Sharp, inspect the boxwork mineral ridges, and return to Base Camp safely.

    1. 3D Street View Navigation: Click directly on the Martian ground (follow the white chevron on the surface) to glide forward like Google Street View, press W / A / S / D or the on-screen D-Pad, or click any floating landmark pin.
    2. Live Sunlight & Scrubbing: Drag the Elevation Scrubber or click the Route Map to jump along the 40 km study area, and adjust the Sunlight & Dust Storm sliders in real time.
    3. 2D Orbital Map Planner: Switch to Map Planner in the top bar at any time to edit stops or inspect NASA MOLA & THEMIS layers.
    Challenge rules followed: authentic Mount Sharp boxwork science target, three planetary data types (MOLA elevation, THEMIS thermal inertia, optical imagery), hazard-aware routing under 15 degrees, and a 45-minute emergency walkback air reserve.
